# Shipping Policy
This is a fictional policy for the OrderDesk demonstration.

- Orders are normally processed within 1 to 2 business days.
- Standard shipping typically takes 3 to 5 business days after dispatch.
- Estimated delivery dates are estimates, not guaranteed delivery commitments.
- Tracking information may take 24 to 48 hours to update after dispatch.
- Customers should contact support if tracking remains unchanged beyond 48 hours.
- A delayed shipment does not automatically qualify for a refund.
- Support agents must use verified order records when communicating order status.
