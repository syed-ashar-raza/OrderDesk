# Billing Policy
This is a fictional policy for the OrderDesk demonstration.

- Customers reporting duplicate charges should provide their order ID and payment details through the approved secure support channel.
- A pending card authorization may temporarily appear alongside a completed charge.
- Pending authorizations are generally released by the payment provider within 5 to 10 business days.
- Actual settlement and release times depend on the customer's bank or payment provider.
- Support agents must not claim that a duplicate charge has been reversed without verified confirmation.
- Unresolved duplicate charges must be referred to the billing support team.
- Customers must never share full card numbers, passwords, or security codes in support tickets.
