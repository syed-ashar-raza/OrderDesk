# Refund Policy
This is a fictional policy for the OrderDesk demonstration.

- Orders with Delivered or Cancelled status qualify for refund review.
- Refund review eligibility does not mean a refund has been approved or processed.
- For delivered orders, customers should request a refund review within 30 calendar days of delivery.
- Returned items must be unused and include their original packaging, unless the item is defective.
- Refund approval requires review by the support team.
- After approval, refunds are issued to the original payment method.
- Refund processing typically takes 5 to 10 business days after approval.
- Support agents must never claim that a refund was approved or processed without verified confirmation.
